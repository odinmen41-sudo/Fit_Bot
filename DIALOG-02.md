# DIALOG-02 — Свободный интеллектуальный диалог

## Product contract

- Proven deterministic and mutation routes retain priority.
- The conversational fallback receives a privacy-safe authoritative projection for nutrition, weight, training, and recovery.
- Values from authoritative readers are facts; causal explanations are labelled as conclusions or hypotheses.
- `MISSING`, `UNAVAILABLE`, and integrity failures are never converted into invented facts.
- Groq may explain or ask one clarification question, but cannot write, correct, or delete data.
- All mutations remain owned by existing domain capture, confirmation, writer, and readback contracts.

## Acceptance

- 60 representative previously-unmapped phrasings with at least 90% profile accuracy.
- Cross-domain context is assembled without internal identifiers.
- One bounded Groq call receives the trusted context separately from user text.
- Context failures degrade to the existing safe fallback without writes.
- Existing protected domain suites remain green.

STAGING deployment and Telegram acceptance require separate approval.
